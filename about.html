What it is and how it works

A single-file, offline editor for interactive "choose your own path" stories. You add pages and choices in your browser, the editor checks that every page and ending can be reached, shows the story as a map, and exports each story as one small game file anyone can play online or offline. The whole editor is one HTML file that runs in your browser; the story stays in your browser storage as you type.

Exact checker

Tries every combination of items, numbers and dice to find dead ends and unreachable endings.

Story map

Several views, from a plain flow to a bird's-eye shape of the whole story.

One small game file

Export makes about 25 to 50 KB for a typical story. It opens in any modern browser.

Stays on your device

Offline, nothing sent anywhere. Save writes a .story.json you can keep.

Why it exists

Writing a branching story is easy; knowing it works is hard. A choice can lead nowhere, an ending can need a key nobody can pick up, and a plain text file warns you about none of it. The Story Editor lets someone who does not program write such a story, be told in plain words what is wrong, and hand over one file with no server, account or install.

Packages you can reuse

MIT, no dependencies, generated from the editor's own sources and tested to give identical answers on hundreds of random stories.

story-formatBranching stories as plain JSON, with a schema and a safe sanitizer.story-checkFinds dead ends and endings no player can reach, and says why.story-playerOne story in, one small offline HTML game out.story-mapLayered, arc, matrix and shape drawings of a story graph.twine-importReads Twine 2, Twee 3 and Twine 1 stories.story-outlineTurns an indented outline into pages and choices.story-diffSays in plain words what one edit did to a story.zip-pageShrinks a page into a self-unzipping page.

Three more (a test runner, a contract-test guide and an AI-collaboration guide) are listed in the README.
