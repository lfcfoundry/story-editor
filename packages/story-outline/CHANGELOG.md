# story-outline changelog

What changed in each version, newest first, one plain line each. The full technical log, with sizes and test browsers, is kept by the maintainers.

## 1.0.1 · 2026-11-06
- A very long run of spaces in a pasted outline no longer makes the reader slow (it took minutes for about a million spaces); same pages and choices.

## 1.0.0 · 2026-10-17
- First release: turns an indented outline into pages and choices, or into a whole story in one call. It tells you in plain words about anything it could not use. It is 6.3 KB gzipped.
